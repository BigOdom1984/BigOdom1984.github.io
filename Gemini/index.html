The Person Behind the Pixels

I'm Greg Odom—independent app developer, founder of CashDublo, and the one pair of hands behind every app on this site. I take ideas from a sketch to an app people can hold: design, code, testing, release, and everything after.

A disabled veteran and Air Force veteran, I hold a degree in Interdisciplinary Studies from UNLV. My computer science education was self-taught, learned by building. Today, I write the apps, the desktop programs, and the servers behind them for Android, Mac, and Windows, with iPhone and Linux on the way.

Home base is Cambodia. I build while traveling Southeast Asia. The places and languages aren't decoration—they're why my apps speak 14 languages and work when the internet doesn't. Never get hotel search anxiety again. That is the promise of my Travel Journal.
