Travel Journal Web.

Travel Journal's web face — the phone app's features in a browser, with your own account: build a story from a trip's photos, confirm each piece with a swipe, and keep your stories on a globe.

Built — dormant · DEVELOPMENT

Travel Journal is the one CashDublo app with a website back end and Google sign-in (optional, never forced). This is its web face: the phone app's features in a browser, with real accounts, stories, review swipes, voice notes, and a globe.

Its own README states its state plainly: built and dormant (2026-11-10). Until three keys are set at deploy time, there is no sign-in and no story can be saved, and its pages show a plain “not connected yet” box. Nothing is faked in the meantime.

No live address yet — the site wakes when its three deploy keys are set. Its state above is from its own repository, checked 2026-11-15.
