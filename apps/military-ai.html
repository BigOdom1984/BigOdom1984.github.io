CashDublo application overview
MA

Military AI

Search and chat over documents published by armed forces and organized protective forces around the world.

Version TBATESTINGPLATFORM TBA

What it is designed to do

  • Choose a force and branch, then ask questions about its documented organization, conduct, logistics and planning.
  • Answers are meant to name the source document and its date.
  • The design aims to include every organized force, but only documented coverage is claimed.

Current status

  • The README marks the app TESTING. The AI model fitting comes after training.
  • The registry starts with U.S. forces. Navy coverage is documented as partial; full coverage is not yet claimed.
  • Version and supported platforms: TBA in the README and SPEC.

Source and availability

The description and status on this page follow the app repository's README and SPEC. A design or testing label does not mean the app is available to download or ready for customers.
