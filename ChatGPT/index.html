Greg Odom · Big Odom
The person behind the pixels

Built from wherever life takes me.

I'm Greg Odom, an independent app developer and founder of Cash to Blow. I take ideas from sketches through development, testing and release, aiming to make useful software that solves real problems.

I served in the United States Air Force and now live and travel throughout Southeast Asia, with Cambodia as home base. The people, languages and places across Cambodia, Laos, Thailand and beyond shape how I think about accessibility and everyday technology.

I also make music and record as Big Odom. Software and music are different ways to create, experiment and connect.
