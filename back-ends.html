CashDublo Enterprise · Category 03

Back Ends.

The servers, services, and systems that make CashDublo applications and front ends work. This page is the inventory and architecture map—not a claim that every listed service is already deployed.

Back ends provide shared capabilities to applications and interfaces. Some products run locally and need no server; others use a shared CashDublo service or a dedicated back end. Each service must be marked by its actual implementation and deployment evidence.

Inventory rule: “Architecture component” means the system belongs in the enterprise map. It does not mean it is built, connected, secure, or live. Promote a component to “implemented” or “production” only after repository, configuration, deployment, and health evidence are checked. Keep credentials and secrets out of this public page.
