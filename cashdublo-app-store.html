CashDublo App Store

A planned platform for developers to present their apps, distribute approved builds, manage customer purchases and subscriptions, handle licensing, and deliver updates through one coordinated service.

Development and availability status: to be confirmed

What the platform is designed to provide

App catalogue

Product pages, app metadata, platform compatibility, release channels and approved downloads.

Commerce and licensing

One-time purchases, subscriptions, receipts, refunds, entitlements and license status.

Distribution and updates

Version manifests, package integrity checks, staged releases, device enrollment and update delivery.

A one-time $19.99–$99.99 edition may suit a standalone local utility, but hosted commerce, licensing, storage, customer accounts and update delivery have ongoing costs. The recommended model for this platform is a recurring subscription, with annual billing discounted. Exact limits and processor fees must be disclosed before sale.

Security and customer protections

Signing operations are handled only through the designated protected service. This public page intentionally contains no private signing material or operational key details. Customer access is controlled by documented entitlements, notices, grace periods and appeal procedures. Local customer data must not be destroyed or held hostage when a hosted entitlement is suspended.

Lawful criticism, negative reviews and complaints are not grounds for suspension or fees. Enforcement is limited to genuine material breaches such as fraud, security abuse or unlawful use, under clear published terms.

Availability

This is a product and pricing outline, not a live storefront or checkout. Do not treat these plans as purchasable until the platform, payment processing, licensing, support terms and release status have been verified.
