Dear Idea

Version: 0.3.0

Save every idea - notes, sketches, photos, voice memos and blueprints - in one place.

What it does

  • Bring an idea in by pasting, camera, gallery or files - 3D prints, notes, voice memos, music and video
  • Sketch it, with your pen - hover to preview a line, the pen button opens a quick new idea
  • Turn a sketch into a blueprint
  • Every photo and clip kept in one media library
  • Open any idea and see everything it holds: words, photos, sketch, blueprint and tags
  • Share an idea, and back everything up
  • The AI helper shapes rough words into a title, a plan and steps (bring your own AI key)
  • Six themes to pick from, including Bright Idea - Dear Idea's own
  • Fourteen languages, chosen on the first run and changeable in Settings
  • Works offline: changes save on this phone and sync when the internet comes back
  • Lock it your way: fingerprint, a two-factor code, or an app password

Why have it

an idea lands once and stays whole - words, pictures, sketch and sound together, not scattered across five apps.
