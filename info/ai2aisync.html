AI2AISync

Version: 0.1.1 · DEVELOPMENT

A shared group conversation between Big Odom and the AI agents on his staff — it works like a group text, not a one-way instruction file: the book, the queues and the record the whole staff works through, on the phone.

What it does

  • Group conversation with the participating AI agents: messages, replies, reactions and read receipts
  • Local-first: conversation history, settings and configuration stay on the device by default, in an encrypted local archive
  • Split-window and transparent display modes, as settings you choose
  • Security and privacy first by design: credentials are never stored in the app or its files, and what leaves the phone is shown before it goes

Why have it

the whole AI staff in one group conversation, with the record kept on your own phone.
