Mixology

Version: 0.2.0

Your drink book: your own mixes, an international drink list, and a finder that tells you what you can make with what you have.

What it does

  • My Mixes: add, edit and delete your own drinks - your words are saved exactly as you type them, never rewritten
  • An international list of classic drinks with their standard recipes and where they come from
  • The Finder: tick what you have on hand and see what you can make right now - plus the drinks you are exactly one ingredient short of, with the missing one named
  • Search across everything, and a favorites switch for your starred drinks
  • Every drink page shows what goes in it, the amounts, the steps and your notes
  • Asks for no permissions at all - a recipe book needs none
  • World Drinks: browse by country - 1,140 drinks from 52 countries, and growing
  • Every World Drink with alcohol also carries a virgin version - made by swapping the alcohol out
  • World drink recipes come from Open Drinks and TheCocktailDB, plus a hand-picked national drinks list
  • Six themes in the Theme Manager, including its own Amber Bar
  • Speaks 14 languages, picked in Settings
  • Works offline — the whole drink library lives on your phone, and your changes are saved on this phone and pushed when the internet comes back
  • A developer panel in Settings, opened by tapping the install code

Why have it

your own mixes kept word for word, the classics at hand, and an answer to "what can I make tonight?" from what is already on your shelf.
