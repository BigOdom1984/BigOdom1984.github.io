Cash Portfolio

SOURCE: ANDROID 0.4.1

Watch your stock portfolio, live prices and price alerts.

What it does

  • Live stock prices with your own free Finnhub key, with backup price sources if one is down
  • Price alerts you set yourself, so the app tells you when a stock hits your price
  • Load your own stock list from a CSV file, and save it back out
  • Money Mood: the app colors itself with how your money is doing
  • Six themes in the Theme Manager, including its own Trading Gold
  • Speaks 14 languages, picked in Settings
  • Offline, it shows your last real prices with the time they were fetched - a stale price is never shown as live, and the AI chat waits until you are back online
  • A built-in help section with answers to common questions

Why have it

your whole portfolio in one calm place, with live prices and your own alerts - and your list stays on your phone.
