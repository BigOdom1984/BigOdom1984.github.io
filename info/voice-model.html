VoiceModel

SOURCE: ANDROID 0.1.0 · Backend

Build your own voice. Paste a link to a video or sound you own, pick the best parts in the clip editor, and train a voice that can speak new words in your voice — or record your voice in the app, or bring your own sound file.

What it does

  • Three ways in: a link (Android and desktop), recording in the app, or importing your own sound file — each way has its own signed text
  • The clip editor lets you mark the good parts of a sound; only the parts you mark are training material
  • Before the first training, three records stand in order: the liability acceptance, the per-voice consent ("This voice is mine, or I have the right to use it"), and the signed text for the path you used — your initials are your signature
  • Before training, the app checks the rule book for your place and names the place whose rule rules; where the book has no sourced rule, the strictest default applies and the app says so
  • Every sound a trained voice makes is labeled AI-generated and carries the made-by-VoiceModel mark; stock voices — voices that clone nobody — speak on the phone itself
  • Your voice is yours: it belongs to the account that made it, it is never shared, it never trains anything else, and you can delete it at any time
  • Source audio passes through memory only and is never stored

Why have it

a voice that is provably yours — your consent on record first, your rules checked first, and every sound it makes labeled as made.
