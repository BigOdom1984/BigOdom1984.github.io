Hit Record

SOURCE: ANDROID 0.3.0

An AI DJ and recording partner that remembers your whole songwriting process.

What it does

  • Press record, freestyle a few bars, stop - every take is saved with its time and its sound
  • Come back days later and add more - takes are grouped into sessions, so you see how the song grew
  • Your words are kept exactly as you write them - slang is never corrected or rewritten
  • The app proposes correction or new lines for each take - you confirm with one tap, and old takes are never deleted quietly
  • A built-in starter beat loop plays while you record, so you write over the beat
  • Ask the DJ (rhyme counts, bar counts, repeated lines) - answered from your lyric sheet only, with your own key
  • Learn the Industry: a teaching library of 56 topics in plain words - royalties, the industry, and copyright fully written (26 lessons), read or listen with no internet
  • Ask the DJ's Lessons mode answers from the written lessons only - it never improvises law, rates, or advice
  • Role Model topics (work ethic, money, contracts, negotiation) wait behind a Settings switch as named TBA lessons until they are written
  • Voice synthesis - Ask the DJ can speak in a trained voice (premium feature, TBA)
  • Share the lyric sheet with one tap
  • Automatic speech-to-text is on the way (TBA)

Why have it

you never lose a bar. Every take, every fix, every session - kept in order until the song is done.
