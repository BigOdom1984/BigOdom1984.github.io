Dear Idea Desktop

MAC & WINDOWS 0.1.0

Dear Idea on your computer — a window program for Mac and Windows that holds the same idea library as the Dear Idea phone app: ideas with words, photos, sketches, voice notes, video clips, 3D print files and blueprint plans.

What it does

  • One library, shared with the phone: both read and write the exact same backup file — export on the phone, open the backup here; save a backup here, restore it on the phone
  • A searchable list of your ideas, with a kind filter: Idea, Sketch, Project, Event, Problem, Blueprint
  • Each idea in full: its words, photos, its sketch drawn out, the blueprint with steps in order, materials and date, the media on the idea, and its tags
  • New idea, Edit and Delete — all saved to the library file right away
  • Export one idea as a plain text file, in the same words the phone app sends when you share an idea
  • A full Help window, and an About window that shows the exact place the library file lives on your computer
  • Photos show here when the same file is also on this computer; otherwise the name is listed, so nothing is a surprise — video and audio play on the phone

Why have it

your ideas on the big screen, in the same library as your phone — never kept in two places, never out of step.
