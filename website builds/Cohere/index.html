About Greg Odom

I'm Greg Odom — independent app developer, founder of CashDublo, and the one pair of hands behind every app on this site. Built from wherever life takes me. My degree is in Interdisciplinary Studies from UNLV, and my computer science is self-taught. I served in the United States Air Force, and today my home base is Cambodia. My apps speak fourteen languages and work offline because they're built for the world I live in.

I also make music and record as Big Odom. Code and songs are the same habit for me: make something honest, finish it, and put it in people's hands.

Philosophy

Room for different paths. To each their own. Live and let live. Travel teaches you that people build good lives in many different ways. I'm interested in useful tools, honest work, new places, good music, and the freedom to choose your own direction.
