Choose how you use it.

Clear, proposed prices for the CashDublo app family — from simple one-time tools to memberships for apps with ongoing hosting, AI or cloud costs.

Pricing status: estimated, not published. Every amount below is a suggested starting price in USD, not a live store price or a confirmed charge. “Estimated price” is shown on every paid app. Final prices should be checked against hosting, AI usage, payment fees, app-store commissions, taxes and regional purchasing power before launch.
