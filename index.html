Greg Odom · Big Odom
The person behind the pixels

Built from wherever life takes me.

I'm Greg Odom — independent app developer, founder of CashDublo, and the one pair of hands behind every app on this site. I take an idea from a sketch to an app people can hold: design, code, testing, release, and everything after.

My degree is in Interdisciplinary Studies — business and communications — from UNLV, and it shows in how I work: start with the person, then the problem. My computer science came the other way: self-taught, learned by building. Today I write the apps, the desktop programs, and the server behind them myself, for Android, Mac, and Windows, with iPhone and Linux on the way.

Before all this, I served in the United States Air Force. These days home base is Cambodia, and I build while traveling Southeast Asia. The places and languages here aren't decoration — they're the reason my apps speak fourteen languages and keep working when the internet doesn't.

I also make music and record as Big Odom. Code and songs are the same habit for me: make something honest, finish it, and put it in people's hands.
