Why have it: your whole portfolio in one calm place, with live prices and your own alerts - and your list stays on your phone.

Testra Accuracy

Pick any topic. The app writes a test on it, you answer, and it grades your accuracy.

  • Type any topic you want to be tested on, or pick a ready-made one
  • Two styles: Scholar (hard questions) or Just for Fun (easier ones)
  • Test sizes from 5 questions up to a 500-question Marathon - big tests are written in batches and can be paused and picked up later
  • Four kinds of questions: multiple choice, true or false, short answer and essay
  • Every question is made with its own answer key, so grading never guesses
  • At the end: your accuracy score, a letter grade, and a review of every question
  • History keeps every finished test, so you can watch your accuracy climb
  • Questions are written with your own key, which is kept only on your phone

Why have it: turn anything you are learning into a real test in minutes, and prove to yourself what you actually know.

Hit Record

  • A built-in starter beat loop plays while you record, so you write over the beat
  • Ask the DJ (rhyme counts, bar counts, repeated lines) - answered from your lyric sheet only, with your own key
  • Share the lyric sheet with one tap
  • Automatic speech-to-text is on the way (TBA)

Why have it: you never lose a bar. Every take, every fix, every session - kept in order until the song is done.

For your computer

The flagship desktop app that feeds your phone.

Apple2Player

Move your Apple Music playlists and songs from your Mac to your Android phone.

  • Reads your playlists straight from Apple Music and pushes the songs to your phone at full cable speed
  • Your Player app picks the playlists up on its own - no manual import
  • Apple Music star ratings are written into your MP3s as they travel, so Player shows them right away
  • Never sends the same song twice - a song already on the phone is skipped and noted
  • Purge: find double copies on the phone, and clear only the true extras - never the last copy
  • Fix Tags: check the songs in any folder and fix their tags, finding songs by how they sound, by their tags, or by file name
  • Every tag fix is backed up first, checked after writing, and logged - a star rating inside a song is never changed
  • Also runs on Windows

Why have it: your Apple Music library, on your Android phone, with its playlists and star ratings intact - in minutes, not an afternoon of dragging files.

How updates work

App updates come from the CashDublo updates shelf - the public cashdublo-updates repo on GitHub.

Updates travel as small patches, not whole new apps: your app saves your settings, builds the new version on your phone, checks it, and only then installs it. If anything does not check out, the update stops safely and your app stays as it was.

Tools

Small helper programs that run on your own computer.

Change App Icons

Swap an app's icon on GitHub, with a backup saved first so you can undo it. The new picture must be a real PNG or JPEG at the exact same size, and after sending, the program reads the icon back and puts the original back on its own if it does not match.

CashDublo Coder

A coding helper that runs on your own computer: ask the AI (a small model on your own machine, offline after a one-time download), browse your GitHub repos and send a file to it, and jump to the docs and forums that help you build.

Script Installer

Lists your build scripts in one window with a plain line on what each one does, and runs the one you pick - in its own windows, or in the terminal pane inside the app.

GitHub HQ

The home screen for your GitHub repos. It opens on your repos - every one of them, with its version, its build scripts and its newest finished build - and can pull, clone and build them from one place.

App Translator

Turns the CashDublo apps into other languages - Khmer, Lao and Thai first. One AI translates each line and a second AI checks it by translating it back; only lines that pass the check are ever shipped, and the rest are held for review.

Build Manager

Builds every CashDublo app from one place: pick an app or build them all, watch the run live, and see what got built - from real build records, never guesses.

Rebuild-All

Keeps every CashDublo repo safe: one small backup file holds the whole set, and the rebuild brings every repo back with only the program and the backup - no GitHub, no sign-in, no internet.
