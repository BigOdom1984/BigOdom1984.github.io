VoiceModel

Full information

  • The clip editor lets you mark the good parts of a sound; only the parts you mark are training material
  • Before the first training, three records stand in order: the liability acceptance, the per-voice consent ("This voice is mine, or I have the right to use it"), and the signed text for the path you used — your initials are your signature
  • Before training, the app checks the rule book for your place and names the place whose rule rules; where the book has no sourced rule, the strictest default applies and the app says so

  • Your voice is yours: it belongs to the account that made it, it is never shared, it never trains anything else, and you can delete it at any time
  • Source audio passes through memory only and is never stored

Why have it: a voice that is provably yours — your consent on record first, your rules checked first, and every sound it makes labeled as made.

Apple2Player

Move your Apple Music playlists and songs from your Mac or Windows computer to your Android phone.

Full information
  • Reads your playlists straight from Apple Music and pushes the songs to your phone at full cable speed
  • Your Player app picks the playlists up on its own - no manual import
  • Apple Music star ratings are written into your MP3s as they travel, so Player shows them right away
  • Never sends the same song twice - a song already on the phone is skipped and noted
  • Purge: find double copies on the phone, and clear only the true extras - never the last copy
  • Fix Tags: check the songs in any folder and fix their tags, finding songs by how they sound, by their tags, or by file name
  • Every tag fix is backed up first, checked after writing, and logged - a star rating inside a song is never changed
  • Song labels: in the background, a few songs a minute, the app works out whether each song is explicit or clean and writes the answer into the moods file Player already receives
  • A song no source can settle is counted as not checked - never as clean - and you can set a song's label by hand
  • Mood ideas: guesses from a song's genre and speed, each with its reason showing, land on a song only when you say yes
  • The WiFi Transfer password is hidden as you type it
  • Also runs on Windows

Why have it: your Apple Music library, on your Android phone, with its playlists and star ratings intact - in minutes, not an afternoon of dragging files.

Change App Icons

A window program for Mac and Windows that shows every app in your GitHub repos with its name, its description, and every icon the repo uses — each at the size it is really used at. Pick a new picture to change an icon, or bring an old one back.

  • Before any change, the old icon is saved into a backup file — no backup, no change
  • After sending, the program reads the icon back from GitHub and compares it; if it does not match, the original is put back on its own

  • Sign-in is a GitHub token you paste once; it never leaves your computer except to GitHub

Why have it: change an app's icon with the risk taken out — backed up first, read back and checked after, and undone on its own if the change does not take.

App Translator

A Mac desktop app that turns the CashDublo apps into other languages. One engine translates each line, a second engine translates the result back, and only lines that pass the check are ever shipped.

Full information
  • Languages built in: Khmer, Lao and Thai — the language list is one table in the app, so a new language is one new row
  • A line counts as verified only when the back-translation matches well, when every pinned glossary word survived word for word, and when the result is a real translation — not the English handed back
  • Flagged lines are kept out of the language file and listed in a review report with the attempt, the back-translation, the match score, and the plain reason
  • The window shows the real coverage line per language — how many of the app's lines are verified, as a count and a percentage
  • The translator and the checker must be different engines: set up the same, the app warns and refuses to mark anything verified in that setup
  • The English source file is never changed by a run, and an app whose words are not in a string file is named plainly as not translation-ready yet
  • A target app falls back to English for any line that is not in its language file yet

Why have it: translations that are proven before they ship — so people who do not speak English get words that were checked, not guessed.
